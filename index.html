Meet Your Physiotherapist

Compassionate care backed by academic excellence

Dr. Vanshika Wayal brings academic excellence and clinical expertise directly to your home, helping you recover with dignity through trusted physiotherapy in Buldhana.

As a licensed physiotherapist registered with the Maharashtra State Council of Occupational Therapy, she specializes in providing personalized, evidence‑based physical therapy services for patients in Buldhana. Her clinical philosophy centers on restoring functionality and movement by combining manual therapy, electrotherapy, and customized exercise therapy.

Home‑based care ensures that patients recover in their natural environment, reducing hospital‑acquired infection risks and scheduling stresses.
